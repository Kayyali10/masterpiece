About us

Understanding Wildlife Starts Here.

WildGuard helps you discover wildlife found in Jordan, including snakes, scorpions, spiders, and other creatures. Our goal is to make wildlife information accessible and help people understand how to interact safely with nature.

We aim to raise awareness, encourage responsible wildlife reporting, and connect people with reliable information to help protect both communities and wildlife.

How it works

  1. Create your account Register for a WildGuard account to explore wildlife information and access the platform's features.
  2. Discover Wildlife Explore information about local species, their habitats, and their characteristics.
  3. Report an Encounter Share a wildlife sighting with a photo, location, and description.
  4. Learn and Stay Safe Follow safety guidelines and explore educational resources about wildlife.
